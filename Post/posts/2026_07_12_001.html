More Apple Devices Hit by Activation Errors

12 July, 2026

Today, four additional devices have been reported to be unable to activate:

  • iPhone 2G
  • iPhone 3G
  • iPhone 3GS
  • iPad (2010) (Cellular)

The impact of the activation issue on these devices is less significant than on the devices listed in the previous post, as they can all be hacktivated using Legacy iOS Kit.
