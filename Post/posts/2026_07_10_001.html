Apple Fixes the Restore Issue for Legacy Devices

10 July, 2026

Today, Apple resumed signing firmware for the devices that were affected yesterday.

The affected devices are:

  • iPhone 4 (iPhone3,3), iOS 7.1.2
  • iPhone 4s, iOS 6.1.3/8.4.1/9.3.5/9.3.6
  • iPhone 5 (GSM and CDMA), iOS 8.4.1/10.3.3/10.3.4
  • iPhone 5c (GSM and CDMA), iOS 10.3.3/10.3.4

  • iPad 2 (iPad2,3 CDMA), iOS 6.1.3/8.4.1/9.3.5/9.3.6
  • iPad 3 (iPad3,2 GSM), iOS 8.4.1/9.3.5/9.3.6
  • iPad 4 (iPad3,5 and 3,6), iOS 8.4.1/9.3.5/9.3.6
  • iPad mini (iPad2,6 and 2,7), iOS 8.4.1/9.3.5/9.3.6

The incident has now been resolved.
